# Hand-off

The active architecture has been reset from source-complete ECL forecasting to
capture-complete observed-world learning. Do not restore the retired online ECL
interpreter or make its coverage a dataset-admission requirement.

The immediate deliverable is one complete original-retail Wine Lunatic route:
all six stages, HIT continuation, zero Bomb, immutable policy, complete factual
frames/transitions, and no infrastructure failure. Validate Practice Stage 4,
5, and 6 first because they bound failures cheaply.

Treat gameplay HITs as baseline measurements. Fix only demonstrated generic
capture, geometry, timing, action-delivery, corpus-integrity, or accounting
defects. Everything about where and how to dodge belongs to data and learning.

After the route baseline passes, freeze an algorithm-independent corpus
inventory, run the serial/parallel Wine differential, collect independent full
episodes, and start the smallest learnability experiment preregistered in
`paper/main.tex`.
