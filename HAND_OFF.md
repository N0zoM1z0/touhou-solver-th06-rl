# Hand-off

The active architecture has been reset from source-complete ECL forecasting to
capture-complete observed-world learning. Do not restore the retired online ECL
interpreter or make its coverage a dataset-admission requirement.

The scientific goal is complete-route Lunatic NMNB probability. The first
optimization claim is narrower: minimize expected undiscounted physical HIT
count over complete, zero-Bomb episodes. Expected HIT count is a dense surrogate
for early learning and is not assumed to rank policies identically to NMNB.
First-HIT survival curves are diagnostics until a separate objective experiment
is preregistered and has nondegenerate coverage.

The first complete original-retail Wine Lunatic route is now immutable E0
evidence at commit `af9900524520b72934a4c55e2f44118f88094633`: all six
stages, HIT continuation through 108 HITs, zero Bomb, 135,561 factual frames,
135,560 transitions, and zero infrastructure failure. Practice Stages 4, 5,
and 6 also completed and passed their independent audits.

Treat gameplay HITs as baseline measurements. Fix only demonstrated generic
capture, geometry, timing, action-delivery, corpus-integrity, or accounting
defects. Everything about where and how to dodge belongs to data and learning.

The fixed-seed serial/parallel Wine differential for the frozen 20%
uniform-shield exploration policy was executed at commit
`76782a4f37e0d12a9a2384561b53e68ceaf998ae`. All three Stage 4 episodes passed
their individual integrity, geometry, latency, and cleanup audits, but the
serial and concurrent HIT counts were 16, 10, and 13 and their factual
trajectories differed. The exact gate therefore rejected the pool. Do not use
it for parallel collection and do not reinterpret individually valid episodes
as proof of equivalence.

No additional corpus collection or training has been started. The next active
implementation is E2/L0 only:

1. derive one learner decision row from an actual immutable-policy invocation
   to the next invocation or physical episode terminal, rather than treating
   every raw frame as a new intervention;
2. preserve proposed, published, sampled, and executed action identity, the
   complete behavior distribution, input-lease elapsed frames, exclusions, and
   interval HIT cost;
3. prove raw linkage, propensity validity, feature leakage rejection, and exact
   complete-episode HIT conservation with tests and two independent feature
   builders.

After E2 passes, first collect an initial serial complete-episode inventory with
unchanged retail clock/update semantics and the frozen 20% uniform
observed-shield mixture. Then run the
action-frequency/reactive controls, current-observation behavior cloning, one
short-history ablation, and then one offline expected-HIT value method. Freeze
the inventory size, seeds, stopping rule, and whole-episode split before the
first collection run or fit. Do not start with IQL/CQL sweeps, Deep Sets, a
recurrent policy, survival-Q, ensembles, active collection, OPE, learned
dynamics/MPC, or SABER-style branching. Each is admitted only by a measured
failure of the preceding simpler experiment.

The current `PolicyContext` is intentionally sufficient only for the scalar
baseline. Do not widen it until an offline held-out experiment shows that one
specified portable history improves a factual target and offline/online feature
parity can be enforced. Exact-prefix Wine branching is not an available
primitive: first prove serial same-seed, same-policy, same-action-prefix factual
and input-delivery digest equality. Preserve the negative parallel result, keep
its equality criterion unchanged, and do not add stage-specific gameplay logic.
