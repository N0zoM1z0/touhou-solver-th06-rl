action-relative counterfactual support for the three Wine sub10 winners,
without treating Linux headless outcomes as promotion evidence?

hypothesis or determine experiment order.  They cannot activate a policy.

## Generic checkpoint selection

For each seed, run the frozen policy from source stage entry until the first
physical terminal, native-authority failure, or tick limit.  Eligible states
must satisfy all of:

- automatically derived source context
  `boss:0:sub10:life_cb14:timer_cb13:nonspell`;
- boundary reserve at most 16 pixels;
- at least 384 bullets and zero lasers;
- at least five locally admissible native-safe actions;
- no Bomb and no benchmark authority-release action.

Select exactly the latest eligible state in that seed.  If a seed has no such
state, record it as missing; do not replace the seed or loosen the selector.
Replay the selected prefix from stage entry and require byte-logical checkpoint
identity plus exact reproduction of every preceding frozen-policy action.

## COW and support gate

At each valid checkpoint, branch every action in its complete current local
native-safe set.  The forced first action is followed only by immutable frozen
UCB decisions, with fresh native certification on every source tick.  Stop a
branch on the first physical HIT, authority failure, natural terminal, or 600
ticks.  Rank outcomes by the existing robust tiers: completion, no HIT,
survival ticks, bucketed minimum safe-set width, then bucketed boundary
reserve.

Candidate construction remains locked unless all conditions hold:

1. at least three odd and three even seeds provide valid checkpoints;
2. at least two odd and two even seeds have a unique robust non-incumbent
   winner;
3. a generic action-relative candidate fitted using Wine plus odd-seed rows
   improves over the incumbent on at least two even-seed rows and is worse on
   none;
4. the candidate uses no frame, RNG seed, checkpoint identity, exact frontier
   mask, or hand-authored phase script;
5. the resident default is always the incumbent and intervention is restricted
   to the predeclared sub10 geometry with the winner present in the current
   local safe set.

Passing authorizes at most three tiny candidate definitions and one separate
factual-prefix shadow audit.  It does not authorize active Wine input.  Any
candidate that later survives shadow and alternating first-failure canary must
still improve complete natural original-retail Wine Stage 6 HIT count before
promotion.
