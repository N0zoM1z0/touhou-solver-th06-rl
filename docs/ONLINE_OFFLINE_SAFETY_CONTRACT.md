# Online/offline control contract

## Claim boundary

The online shield certifies one narrow statement:

> For the recorded player path and supported short-horizon kinematics of
> already-instantiated bullets, lasers, and enemy bodies, this action has no
> modeled overlap within the shield horizon.

It does not certify safety from hazards that do not yet exist in the captured
root. It must expose its horizon, geometry margin, primitive set, and empty-set
events in every decision record. Calling this set globally safe is forbidden.

Unknown ECL behavior is neither interpreted nor silently declared harmless.
It is outside the shield claim and is handled as future risk by the policy. The
source-order audit for E1 establishes that a newly born hazard can enter the
collision chain after player motion and before the next completed root. This is
an explicit partial-observability boundary, not a mandate for online ECL
forecasting; the corpus preserves its outcomes for history-based learning.

## Online sequence

1. Suspend the exact Wine game process.
2. Capture one coherent player/object/resource/input root.
3. Project only instantiated physical hazards for a fixed short horizon.
4. Compute the observed-shield-admissible Bomb-free actions with bounded native
   geometry work.
5. Let an immutable policy rank or sample only those actions.
6. Revalidate the paused epoch, publish to the exact PID, and resume.
7. Capture the next root and record the factual transition.

Coherent roots, process ownership, memory layouts, geometry of observed
objects, Bomb exclusion, and action witnessing are correctness authority.
Learned value, support, confidence, ECL coverage, and source prediction are not.

## Offline freedom

Offline code consumes only executed transitions and may never manufacture a
counterfactual successor. It may derive decision epochs, portable histories,
auxiliary factual targets, and fitted scores while preserving raw linkage and
complete-episode HIT accounting. Its exported policy is immutable and cannot
add an action rejected by the online shield.

Algorithmic freedom is hypothesis-gated rather than restricted to the weakest
available model. The completed order is causal decision-row audit, one frozen
serial complete-episode inventory, action-frequency/reactive controls,
current-observation behavior cloning, fixed-horizon factual-risk probes, and
one direct Brier-trained action-conditioned hazard model. That direct model
established overall nonlinear action-relative signal but localized its
remaining failure to the baseline-heavy logged action measure. The next
admitted change is one bounded target/behavior-weighted hazard whose target
measure is uniform over the observed-shield set. Exact behavior propensity and
shield-set size may define offline loss and evaluation weights, but may never
become actor inputs. A fixed short-history ablation is admitted only if this
support-corrected current-root boundary still fails; one offline expected-HIT
value method follows only after the representation gate.
Object-set/recurrent encoders, alternative survival objectives, CQL after IQL,
ensembles, OPE, active collection, learned dynamics/MPC, and real-Wine branch
acquisition require separate evidence gates. A learned prediction scores
unknown future risk; it never turns the absence of a predicted birth into an
online safety certificate.

Privileged facts such as stage, spell, ECL, RNG, and addresses may be used to
audit splits or diagnose leakage. They are forbidden deployable actor inputs.

## Failure ownership

- Missing/incoherent frame, invalid geometry decode, wrong successor linkage,
  Bomb, stale publication, or corrupt shard: infrastructure failure.
- Observed-shield replay disagreement: shield implementation failure.
- HIT from an already-observed hazard despite a shield-admissible published
  action: shield counterexample candidate; reproduce and audit geometry/timing.
- HIT involving a hazard first seen after the decision root: unobserved-dynamics
  outcome and evidence for the research lemma, not automatic infra failure.
- HIT with passing contracts: policy outcome; collect and learn.
