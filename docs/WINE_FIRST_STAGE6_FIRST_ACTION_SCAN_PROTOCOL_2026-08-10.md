Wine failure anchor after correcting the missing enemy interpolation state.
This is hypothesis generation only. It is not a fit, a policy evaluation, or
promotion evidence, because every branch uses the offline native teacher after

alternating complete natural original-retail Wine Stage 6 HIT count against the
frozen incumbent, ultimately requiring repeatable 0-HIT clears. Full-Stage HIT
continuation is evaluation-only and never enters training.

and `down_right` at panel-2 r6 run `20260810T134906Z-860850500`, sequence 6354,
retail frame 6999. This is the closest-to-failure row in the final contiguous
