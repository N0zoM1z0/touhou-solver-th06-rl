## Executive diagnosis

The project is not currently blocked by one demonstrated Linux-to-Windows
platform shift. The strongest demonstrated failure is earlier in the chain:
offline/static success frequently does not survive closed-loop Linux-headless
rollout on unseen seeds. Separately, the first original-retail Wine baseline
used the online adaptive UCB policy, not an offline `.joblib` ranker, and had
many HITs. Therefore that run cannot measure offline-to-Wine transfer.

| Question | Current evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the offline learner fit its labels? | Often yes: some held-out imitation, HIT-ranking, and COW metrics are strong. | **Yes, but this is only diagnostic.** |
| Does a strong offline metric imply a better Linux closed-loop policy? | Repeated fresh-seed rollouts regress, sometimes drastically. | **No. This gap is confirmed and large.** |
| Does an offline/distilled policy work in the original game under Wine? | No offline `.joblib` policy was connected to the original-retail Wine runner. | **Unknown; it has not been tested.** |
| Is Linux-headless versus original-retail/Wine drift the current cause? | The two domains have not yet replayed a frozen policy or identical action stream under a comparable contract. | **Unknown. Do not claim either equivalence or harmful drift yet.** |
| Does the current online UCB solve the original game? | Natural Practice Stages 6 through 1 had 58 HITs; a full route had 68. | **No.** |
| Is native geometry still generally wrong? | Several concrete authority bugs were fixed. Corrected exact-state audits find early constructive witnesses and some true late dead ends. | **No general geometry failure is established; individual Wine dead ends still need classification.** |
| Is NMNB established anywhere? | There is no complete full-route no-HIT/no-Bomb result and no promoted model. | **No.** |

The practical diagnosis is:

1. Offline objectives and data coverage still produce closed-loop covariate
   shift and compounding errors. A global tree can memorize a failure repair
   while perturbing many well-supported decisions earlier in the route.
2. The historical state representation omitted some predictive context. Exact
   source audits exposed numeric ECL/source clocks and Boss-relative/contact
   context as useful generic ranking features. New data is required under that
   schema; adding the feature to code does not retrofit old rows.
3. Sparse behavior support, especially in Stage 5, and few complete independent
   route groups make conventional OPE and fitted-Q conclusions unstable.
4. Strict teacher top-1 or unique-best imitation is the wrong primary target.
   Many certified actions are survivable; the target is repeatable stage/route
   survival, future legal-set width, reserve, and HIT count.
5. Original-retail Wine is now a viable online validation environment, but the
   policy-evaluation contract is incomplete: the baseline UCB explored and
   mutated state, and no offline-ranker adapter exists in that path.

In short, saying only "offline training is bad" is too coarse, and saying
"offline-to-online platform shift" is premature. Offline fitting is sometimes
good, **offline-to-headless closed-loop generalization is demonstrably bad**,
and **offline-to-original-retail transfer remains unmeasured**.

## Non-negotiable product and safety boundary

- Native geometry alone constructs the publishable first-action set. Learning
  may rank it but may never enlarge it, weaken margins, bypass fail-close, or
  own collision authority.
- Capture one coherent physical snapshot, construct the bounded native set,
  rank it, then recapture/revalidate immediately before publication.
- Bomb bit `0x02` is forbidden in every mode.
- No movement keyed to frame, seed, counterexample, Boss name, or handwritten
  phase state. Source identity is for partitioning, evaluation, and generic
  model conditioning only.
- No ECL/timeline birth interpretation or beam/tree search in the resident hot
  path. Source information remains lossless offline metadata.
- Default physical runs stop on first HIT, authority failure, or Bomb request.
  HIT continuation is benchmark-only and never training corpus.
- Never launch the Windows game through a PTY. Release input, stop only the
  exact trial PID/prefix, and check leftovers after every run.
- Do not use REA, REA-provided tools, or LeanToken.
- Do not touch unrelated Wine/Codex processes. At handoff there is no TH06
  game, controller, runner, or `Xvfb :97` from this project; generic Wine
  service processes belonging to other work may still exist.

### What Wine still does not answer

There is currently no hot-path plug-in that loads the offline `.joblib`
rankers. `src/th06_rl/policies/adaptive.py` is the deployed online policy; the
distilled-ranker loader exists only in headless/offline scripts. The Wine
controller's `PolicyContext` is also much coarser than the later headless
candidate-profile/source-context feature contract. A feature adapter and
contract audit are required before an offline model can be evaluated fairly.

Setting exploration to zero is insufficient because current UCB still updates
and checkpoints. A model-comparison run first needs immutable evaluation mode:
copied/read-only state, no `observe`, no checkpoint mutation, and asserted
before/after hash equality.

- The first Stage 6 LightGBM distillation had about 99% held-out teacher top-1

  23.3%/3.3%/0% complete-seed held-out top-1 for Stages 3/4/5; unseen-seed

- A Stage 6 route-wide snapshot reached 67.5% held-out robust-action top-1 and

  incumbent's 18,810/20,040.
- A Stage 5 correction improved one seed from 3,252 to 5,415 decisions but
  regressed another from 16,419 to 1,878; aggregate survival fell from 19,671

These are policy-selection regressions with native-legal, Bomb-free actions;

## Exact-state oracle and fixed infrastructure errors

The exact-state feasibility benchmark was the most useful recent work. It
turned apparent "unlearnable last states" into traceable causes and prevents
training on impossible final-frame labels.

Confirmed and fixed issues:

1. Player-aim bullet projection was over-enclosed.
2. Aim state leaked across mutually exclusive candidate paths.
3. Synchronous Linux `STEP` incorrectly inherited the Windows 0--3-tick input
   delivery envelope; synchronous headless delivery is exactly delay 0.
4. Newborn lasers lacked an explicit angle-initialization observation and
   caused false fail-close terminals.
5. Diagnostic preceding-tick `events` was incorrectly included in the
   physical-state digest.
6. The ranker feature contract omitted bounded numeric source clocks and
   current Boss-relative/contact context. These are generic learned features,
   never collision authority or a phase script.

The old claim that seed 113/114 had only one tick of survival was invalidated.
After the aim and delivery fixes, seed 113 `stay + h12` survived 1,200 ticks.
At seed 114 sequence 4,183, both the factual and a higher-reserve alternative
survived 1,200 ticks; the historical continuation reached an empty set later.

Some terminal states are genuinely too late. In later pure-h12 failures,
configured and margin-zero native sets were empty and all 18 source-executed
constant actions physically HIT within four ticks. Those rows diagnose an
earlier planning failure; they are not supervised action targets and do not
authorize weakening margin.

The corrected 240-tick six-checkpoint audit accepted 408/408 continuation
branches over 102 native first actions. Every checkpoint had at least one
witness. On the four discriminative checkpoints, a leave-one-seed-out probe
selected a feasible action 75% of the time from compact features and 100% from
bounded exact-derived features. This tiny sample is evidence of representation
loss, not a generalization claim.

The oracle's key operational rule is: search backward from a HIT/dead end until
the earliest constructive witness appears. Late no-witness states are geometry
diagnostics; earlier witness states are ordinal value supervision. Promotion
still depends on fresh natural closed-loop rollouts.

## Recommended continuation, in order

### P0: restore immutable comparability

1. Regenerate the policy-population index against the exact active source and
   binary. Preserve history; do not silently relabel old weights compatible.
2. Select only one incumbent and one quality-diverse candidate per Stage for
   the first A/B. Identify them by full model SHA, source commit/binary,
   delivery contract, feature schema, and observation-digest contract.
3. Add immutable evaluation mode: exploration 0, copied/read-only state, no
   updates/checkpoint writes, and an asserted equal before/after SHA-256.

### P1: make offline-to-original-retail evaluation real

4. Add a small generic offline-ranker policy plug-in/feature adapter for the
   Windows controller. It must rank exactly the native-gated set and leave
   fresh issue certification unchanged. Audit which later headless features
   can be constructed from one coherent retail snapshot; reject a model when
   its schema cannot be reproduced exactly.
5. First shadow-score the ranker beside the frozen incumbent. Then run paired
   natural Practice continuation trials with immutable policies. Retail RNG is
   not yet controlled, so one unrelated route is not a valid A/B; use repeated
   trials or create an action-stream/replay comparison.

This is the first experiment that can actually answer whether an offline
candidate transfers to original-retail Wine. Until it exists, do not label the
Wine UCB result an offline sim-to-real regression.

### P2: localize online HIT causes

6. Add a bounded lossless pre-HIT/pre-dead-end ring buffer or exact snapshot
   anchors to targeted Wine runs. The previous `--no-corpus` traces are not
   enough for the exact-state oracle. Continuation rows remain
   `training_eligible=false`.
7. Audit Stage 4 first, then Stages 5/6. Classify each sampled neighborhood as:
   capture fail-close; configured-margin-only closure; true margin-zero/source
   closure; earlier constructive policy witness; or delivery discrepancy.
8. Improve coherent capture only while preserving fail-close. Do not hide the
   operational cost of hundreds of release frames by weakening authority.

### P3: conservative learning rather than another global rewrite

9. Build balanced route-wide COW data from both successful incumbent states
   and failure neighborhoods across multiple seeds. Hold out complete seeds.
10. Train robust/survivable ordinal value using survival, minimum future legal
    width, and reserve. Preserve a quality-diverse population.
11. Use a behavior-regularized residual/selector: retain the incumbent unless
    several cross-seed COW-supported models agree that an override is better.
    Always validate the selected weight by fixed same-seed natural panels.
12. Consider conservative Q/IQL/CQL only after action support and terminal
    return diversity improve. Existing FQI/OPE evidence is too unstable to
    justify replacing the current workflow.

### P4: isolate platform drift

13. Replay identical frozen action streams through native Linux, the MinGW
    reconstructed source under Wine, and original retail Wine. Compare
    snapshots/HITs at the earliest divergence. The current retail path cannot
    directly set the source RNG, so an exporter/replay mechanism is needed.
14. Use a real Windows machine for final shadow/canary and NMNB evidence. Wine
    is close validation, not the final equivalence claim.

Do not begin with a larger global fit, a broader hyperparameter sweep, or more
online UCB time. Those repeat already falsified experiment shapes without
distinguishing data, representation, policy, capture, and platform causes.
